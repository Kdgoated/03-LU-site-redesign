About Fran

Francine "Fran" Miller opened Fran's Flowers in 1995 with a mission to craft beautiful, thoughtful bouquets and create connection in her neighborhood. She sources sustainably and enjoys helping customers find the perfect arrangement for every occasion.

When she's not arranging flowers you'll find her volunteering at community gardens and baking for local fundraisers.
